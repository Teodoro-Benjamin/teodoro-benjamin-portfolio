Portfolio Case Study: Cold Calling and Objection Handling
This transcript shows you how patience and respect for prospect timing turn a brush-off into a booked opportunity.
Initial Call
Benjamin: Hi Samantha. My name is Benjamin with MID Construction. Do you have a quick moment?
Samantha: Well hello yes.
Benjamin: Our records show your property has been under construction for the past two months while waiting for building permits. Is that correct?
Samantha: Yes that is true. We have had a hard time waiting for those permits.
Benjamin: MID Construction specializes in expediting permits and managing construction paperwork. Do you have time later today for a brief meeting with our permitting expert?
Samantha: I would love to, but I cannot look at details right now because I am driving. Call me back later.
Benjamin: Drive safely. I will follow up with you later today. Have a great drive.
Callback
Benjamin: Hi Samantha. This is Benjamin following up from our earlier call.
Samantha: Yes this is her. Thank you for calling back and reminding me. I am still interested.
Benjamin: That is great to hear. Send me your email address and I will forward a scheduling link so you can pick a convenient time this week.
Why This Approach Wins
Respecting safety: When you immediately stop pitching upon hearing the prospect is driving, you show genuine care over a quick sale.
Following through: Calling back exactly when requested proves your reliability.
Keeping it simple: Direct questions keep the dialogue moving forward without confusion.
